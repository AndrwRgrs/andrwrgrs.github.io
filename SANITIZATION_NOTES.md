## Disclosure boundary used

The portfolio now treats GlüxKind's public product pages as the maximum disclosure boundary. They describe Rosa's user-facing capabilities, including powered assistance, automatic braking and parking, built-in soothing, app connectivity, remote diagnostics, and software updates:

- [Rosa smart stroller](https://gluxkind.com/rosa-smart-stroller)
- [Rosa FAQs](https://gluxkind.com/rosa-faqs)
- [Rosa shop listing](https://shop.gluxkind.com/products/gluxkind-rosa-smart-stroller)
- [Ella smart stroller](https://gluxkind.com/ella-smart-stroller)

Public product information does not establish Andrew's individual authorship of a feature or authorize disclosure of how it works. The portfolio therefore separates public product context from Andrew's high-level employment contribution.

The Ella page was also reviewed. It publicly describes high-level product positioning, but it does not connect Andrew to those capabilities or authorize disclosure of related engineering work. Ella-specific work is therefore not included in the portfolio.
