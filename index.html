GlüxKind Robotics

Rosa, GlüxKind’s smart-stroller platform

As GlüxKind’s controls subject-matter expert for Rosa, I was directly responsible for bringing powered assistance, automatic braking, and parking from prototype behaviours to production-ready features. I developed and supported the C++ firmware behind these controls and helped move Rosa from prototype-stage development to commercial production.

View Rosa on GlüxKind’s website ↗

Controls features I brought to production:

  • Powered assistance that adapts to terrain and load
  • Automatic braking
  • Parking support

My controls work spanned control design, state estimation, embedded C++ implementation, hardware integration, validation, and production support. Beyond controls, I contributed across Rosa’s broader firmware stack, including audio, Spotify integration, on-device logging, OTA updates, and remote diagnostics.
